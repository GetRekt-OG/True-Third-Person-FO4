# Credits

Special thanks to the authors who made their work available for other modders to
learn from:

- **cravecruncher** — for sharing Independent Weapon Facing – Free Movement Drawn.
  Its camera and movement implementation provided the foundation and reference
  for this project's independent weapon-facing behavior.
- **AozoraCynthiA / CythiaSu** — for sharing [Simple Aim Assist](https://github.com/CythiaSu/Fallout4-SimpleAimAssist).
  Its Fallout 4 targeting, custom-menu lifecycle and world-to-screen implementation
  helped guide the melee target-lock module.
- **Ershin / ersh1** — for sharing [True Directional Movement](https://github.com/ersh1/TrueDirectionalMovement).
  Its third-person movement and target-lock design inspired this project's approach.
- **Mod Configuration Menu contributors** — for the configuration format and settings interface.
- **F4SE, CommonLibF4 AV, fmt and spdlog contributors** — for the
  plugin infrastructure, engine definitions and address research used in development.
- **Flan02** — for sharing Toggle Aim. Its OG and AE sources helped identify the
  ADS timing conflict and informed the compatibility handling. Its assembly hook
  is not copied or patched by this project.

True Third Person is a separate project and is not an official release of these
mods. The marker artwork and ActionScript implementation are specific to this project.

Address-research attribution and its license are retained in `THIRD-PARTY`.

The top-level MIT license does not replace third-party terms. In particular, the
Simple Aim Assist non-commercial license is retained in
`THIRD-PARTY/SimpleAimAssist-LICENSE.md`. Dependency licenses remain with their sources.
